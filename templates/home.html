The problem

Information about hall and venue availability is hard to track when multiple departments need the same facilities. Requests travel by phone, paper and WhatsApp; two events land in the same seminar hall; nobody knows which halls are actually free.

What this system does

  • Centralised, live availability grid for every venue
  • Online reservation requests with event details
  • Database-enforced conflict prevention — no double booking
  • Hall supervisor approval workflow with notes
  • Maintenance / reserved slot blocking
  • Suggests other free halls that fit your crowd
  • Utilisation reports by venue and department, Excel export
